# Code of conduct

We want Scalo to be a project where people are comfortable asking questions, proposing ideas and
disagreeing about technical choices.

**Expected**: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback,
focus on what is best for users and for the project.

**Not accepted**: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing
someone's private information, sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, write to contact@sacha-cohen.fr. Reports are handled confidentially.

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1.
